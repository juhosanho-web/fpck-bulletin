Do You Know This Hope?
"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans for welfare and not for evil, to give you a future and a hope."
— Jeremiah 29:11
In a world of uncertainty and hardship, God offers a hope that does not disappoint. The Bible tells us that every person is loved by God — and that He sent His Son, Jesus Christ, to restore what was broken between us and Him.
  • ✦ God loves you. Before you were born, He knew your name. (Ps. 139:13)
  • ✦ We all need forgiveness. Every person has fallen short of God's glory. (Rom. 3:23)
  • ✦ Jesus is the way. He died and rose again so that we might live. (John 14:6)
  • ✦ You can begin again. Whoever calls on His name will be saved. (Rom. 10:13)
Come and see. We would love to walk this journey with you.
